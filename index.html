Specialization
Generative Engine Optimization

Most SEO teams are still focused entirely on Google's blue links. That's not wrong — but it's incomplete. AI-powered search platforms now answer questions directly, and if your brand isn't the source being cited, you're invisible in those answers.

GEO is about making your content the thing that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity choose to reference. The signals that matter here are different from traditional SEO — it's about topical authority, factual clarity, entity recognition, and content structure that language models can confidently extract and present.

At DemandNXT, this wasn't experimental — it produced 10% of monthly traffic and 40+ SQL leads per month, entirely from AI search. That's a channel most teams are still ignoring.
